Inland services

Precision on every road. Connecting ports, borders, and businesses.

  • Container transport

    Swift intermodal movement between major ports, railheads, and client distribution centers. Dedicated container chassis and specialized trailers handle standard, high-cube, and refrigerated (reefer) containers, with storage yards in Jeddah and Dammam.

  • Cross-border services

    Dedicated routes connect Saudi Arabia to and from the UAE, Bahrain, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Jordan, Egypt, and Syria, with streamlined handovers. Expert navigation of regional customs documentation, tariff codes, and border clearance compliance prevents unnecessary transit delays.

  • Domestic transport

    A modern, well-maintained fleet handles heavy-duty industrial goods, palletized cargo, and high-volume commercial shipments. Your cargo receives 100% dedicated truck space, without consolidation or multiple drop-offs. Streamlined, non-stop routing connects your origin facility or port straight to the final destination across the Kingdom.

  • Break-bulk

    Specialized low-beds, multi-axle trailers, and heavy-lifting equipment engineered for non-containerized, oversized industrial cargo.

  • Automotive business

    Modern, secure multi-car transporters for safe vehicle transit across regional and domestic routes. Rigorous loading, securing, and unloading protocols ensure vehicles arrive pristine and ready for the showroom floor.

Deep local knowledge. Fewer barriers. Smoother trade.
